# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and technically rigorous community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Backing performance and diagnostic claims with reproducible evidence, measurements, and falsifiable hypotheses
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission (or submitting unredacted diagnostic captures containing personal identifiers)
* Encouraging or submitting code that bypasses anti-cheat protections, injects into foreign processes, or introduces telemetry/dark patterns

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer via GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
